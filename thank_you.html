Getting started
1
Download your Google Sheet template
Click the Download Google Sheet Template button in the Your Links section to make a copy of the Match Tracker Pro Google Sheet. This is where all your match data will be stored — securely in your own Google account.
💡
Your sheet will open as "Copy of Match Tracker Pro" — that's just how Google names copies. Feel free to rename it to anything you like (e.g. My Tennis Stats) from Google Sheets without affecting how the app works.
2
Open Match Tracker Pro & connect
Visit the app by clicking the Open Match Tracker Pro button in the Your Links section. On your first visit, instructions display for connecting Match Tracker Pro with your Google Sheet. After that, you're live — no login, no account needed.
