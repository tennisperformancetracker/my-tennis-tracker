What we collect

Match Tracker Pro collects only one type of personal information: your email address, and only if you voluntarily opt in to receive product updates.

We do not collect any other personal information. We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or tracking pixels of any kind.

Your Google Sheet data

Match Tracker Pro connects to a Google Sheet that you own and control. We do not access, read, store, or transmit any data from your Google Sheet. All of your match tracking data remains in your Google account and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

How we use your email

If you opt in, we use your email address solely to send occasional product updates and announcements about Match Tracker Pro. We will never:

  • Sell or share your email with third parties
  • Send you marketing from other companies
  • Use your email for any purpose other than product updates

How we send emails

Updates are sent manually by the Match Tracker Pro team from TennisPerformanceTracker@gmail.com. We do not use bulk email platforms or marketing automation services.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe from updates at any time by replying to any update email and asking to be removed. We will delete your email address promptly upon request.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights.

Children's privacy

Match Tracker Pro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify opt-in subscribers by email before the changes take effect.
